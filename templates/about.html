About DENZ

A Full 3D Virtual AI Assistant Experience

What is DENZ?

DENZ is a groundbreaking 3D virtual assistant that combines cutting-edge web technologies with artificial intelligence to create an entirely immersive, interactive experience. Built with Three.js, WebGL, and custom GLSL shaders, DENZ represents the convergence of advanced 3D graphics and conversational AI.

Unlike traditional interfaces, DENZ exists in a fully 3D environment where every interaction is visually represented through morphing geometries, particle systems, and real-time animations. This creates a truly futuristic and engaging way to interact with artificial intelligence.

3D Capabilities

Morphing Geometry

Real-time procedural geometry that morphs between different shapes. Using advanced vertex shaders and morphing algorithms, objects smoothly transition between spheres, toruses, icosahedrons, and custom geometries.

Particle Systems

GPU-accelerated particle systems capable of rendering 10,000+ particles simultaneously. Each particle has physics simulation, including gravity, wind, and collision detection for realistic motion.

Custom Shaders

Advanced GLSL vertex and fragment shaders for stunning visual effects. Including Phong lighting, Toon shading, volumetric effects, and procedural noise-based animations.

Real-time Rendering

Optimized WebGL rendering achieving 60+ FPS on modern devices. Uses buffer geometry, instanced rendering, and GPU acceleration for maximum performance.

Physics Engine

Integrated physics simulation for interactive 3D elements. Includes rigid body dynamics, collision detection, and constraint-based animation for realistic physical behavior.

WebGL Technology

Cross-platform 3D graphics powered by WebGL and Three.js. Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without requiring plugins or external software.
